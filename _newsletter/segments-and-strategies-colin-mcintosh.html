Do you remember the movie War Dogs? Well that is where this story begins…

Colin is the CEO of Sheets & Giggles. His inspiration for starting a bed sheet business is unexpected, but it fits with his take on the industry.

S&G’s journey is also an examples of how to scale up by doing things that don’t scale, and the importance of interacting with customers by the whole team… not just one department.

There is a lot of good stuff in here about ecommerce, sustainability, and the American consumer. Hope you enjoy!
