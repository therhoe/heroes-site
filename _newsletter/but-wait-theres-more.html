Ok before you call me out that LLMs and MLMs are not the same thing at all, read the lines below and tell me which popular MLM tropes from 90’s don’t apply to both...

“Be your own boss!”

“Achieve financial freedom.”

“What would you do with an extra \$500 - \$1000 a month?”

“Replace your 9-to-5.”

“Work from anywhere, just using your laptop.”

“Make your own hours.”

“Stop letting others steal your dreams.”

“Passive residual income.”

Right?!?! Eerily similar...

Its almost as if selling a dream is an easier way to get rich than providing a product or service. Which is nothing new in the grand scheme of things. This kind of stuff has always existed, and always does well to get its hooks into people.

It’s just more annoying now that it is tech brodhisattvas come back from ai-nlightnement to shill instead of Mary Kay and Tupperware parties.
