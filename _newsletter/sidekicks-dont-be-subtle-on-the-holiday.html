It is that time of the year… that time of the year that we hope businesses hold off on the Christmas music and decor until after Halloween.

Seriously, people need the full time we allot them to enjoy their pumpkin spiced lives.

But that does not mean we can wait to start getting the marketing ready for the big day(s).

Kurt Elster came on the show to talk about how to get our homepages ready for the holidays, and really hammers home the idea that this is not the time of year to be cool or clever.
