It was back in February when I made these. There was another one I made too but it was deleted. I don’t remember what it was for though.

But I clearly remember why I made them.

We were under attack

When this feature in their ai Sidekick was released, I think we all tried it out. And if you did try it, I would love to hear about it.. comment or email me

So I would have tried it out anyway, but the reason that these 2 apps were made was because of one of those frivolous ADA lawsuits.

If you haven’t been through one, here is the lowdown.

Shitty Lawyers

Apparently the firm behind out suit had filed +1000 lawsuits. And their tactic was simple. Automate the filings, scare you, get you to settle. They were looking for a $7500 settlement.

That number is not so scary. I mean, it would completely fuck over a small Shopify store still struggling to get off the ground, but they don’t go after those sties. They wait until you are bringing in a certain amount of revenue or traffic (I don’t know what they track exactly, this is my assumption), and then slap you with one.

ADA Compliance

Trying to make your website accessible to everyone is a good thing. The things we need to do better as website developers for accessibility is not very visible.

Alt Text on images helps screen readers and their users to understand your products better. That is great and easy to do, in theory, but in practice, it can be a huge undertaking. Especially if you didnt take the images yourself… or if you inherited a store, and no one added any Alt Text, you gotta go one by one?

Matrixfy

Best and most used app for bulk editing. You export what you need into a spreadsheet, and then you can edit them easily. Import the file back into Shopify via Matrixify again and it syncs up the changes. Doneskies.

Great for migrations and saving your ass.

I once cleaned up a mess of a blog with great results, but a year later, the company couldn’t find several old blogs that were needed for something different. There was no backup anywhere. The blog was the backup. Fortunately, I used Matrixify and had ALL of those old and deleted blog posts in an excel file… so after the oh shit moment, everything turned out fine.

Media Cleanup

This was an app I found to help with the Alt Text. It helped me get through a lot of files.

I only used it for that month though before downgrading. Well worth the $14.99. Some apps are for projects and not for an on-going need. I wish there were a way for apps like that to have a separate billing type so you dont have to subscribe and then uninstall or downgrade.
