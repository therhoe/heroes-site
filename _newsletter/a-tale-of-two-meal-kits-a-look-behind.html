Selling meal kits seems pretty hardcore.

Both of these guests use the word aggressive to describe how these companies try to get and retain customers, but after hearing from them, I get why…

They also both have very different tales to tell.

Travis, from Kansas, has been using meal kits services for 5 years. It works for him and he enjoys them. He also shares an interesting workaround to getting occasional discounts.

Vince, from France, on the other hand, had a less then optimal experience. He cancelled his subscription before the first box landed on his porch. I am not sure how common that is, but those in these companies should take note.

Hope you enjoy, and if you’ve tried a meal kit, tell us about your experience in the comments!
