On average, 1 in 5 items purchased online were returned in 2021, costing the industry as a whole over 200 million bucks. That staggering amount doesn’t even account for the impact returns has on the environment through emissions, materials used, landfills.

Brian Taylor is the CEO and founder of Hafback, and we get into the facts around these issues and how he is building a marketplace to reverse reverse-logistics and keep returned items moving forward to customers instead of to the landfill.
