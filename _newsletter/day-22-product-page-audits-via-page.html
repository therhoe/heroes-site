Last year when I started thinking about building Mouse Whisperer, I was planning on just making a simple analytics tool for page audits. And that idea came to me as I sat in this very same chair, looking at the results of an AB test I was running…

I had no idea what was going on…

The AB testing tool we used was not cheap, but it was a great app, and showed us exactly what we wanted: conversion data. But it was not enough. Just like with drugs and/or Pringles… once you pop, the fun don’t stop…

I wanted more of the raw data. Sure, I was happy to know that Version A converted at 11% and Version B converted at 15%, but what else was going on?

  • What were customers doing?

  • Did traffic from each source convert the same?

  • Were they clicking on images or links?

  • How much traffic was from this country and how many international visitors?

So I built Mouse Whisperer to answer those questions… and then later added AB testing so I could cancel that AB testing app that was costing several hundred bucks a month.

So what do this little snapshots look like?

Let’s just watch. It’ll be faster.
