It’s easy to make fun of agencies, but there are good ones and bad ones. And from their point of view, they have good clients and bad clients…

However you look at it, it generally comes down to having clear communication and clear goals.

An agency that says we can do everything for you is just as bad as a client whose attitude is I don’t care how you do it, I just want results.
