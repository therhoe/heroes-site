Working from home is not going anywhere. We like it too much. Less commute, less office politics, less pants… whatever the reason, we don’t want to go back to the office.

So it is no wonder that standing desks have become so popular. We need better workstations at home.

In this episode we talk with two standing desks experts about their experience.

Victor is an actual expert in ergonomics who shares with us everything you need to know about shopping for a sit-stand desk. He has great recommendations for each tier in pricing, and what to look for in terms of a suitable height.

And London who can be considered an expert by the sheer amount of time he’s spent shopping for a standing desks. He’s got a great story of his old one going kaputt right in the middle of his first day at a new job. But no worries, his new one is going to last him 13 years.

Hope you enjoy, and if you have a story about your WFH set up or standing desk, let us know in the comments!
