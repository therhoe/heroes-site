Hunters, late night phone calls, and doctor-prescribed ebikes… John has stories.

He is the owner/founder of Ebikegeneration.com, and he has a very specific clientele: men, 55-75, who hunt.

That is not the first type of customer that comes to mind when we think of ebikes. But when most businesses zigged, John zagged, and he captured a niche audience that he’s gotten to know very well.

And that is the key to his success. He got to know the customers on a deeper level than most businesses do. Spending significant amount of time talking with them and learning about their situation, family, health, and finding what was best to help them out.

His approach to marketing is refreshing, and is an incredible lesson in the importance of truly understanding our customers.

Hope you enjoy.
