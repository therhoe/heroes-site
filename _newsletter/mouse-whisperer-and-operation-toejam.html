Operation Toejam

On Day #15 I talked about doing a brand reset for the website that I manage — which is not about redesigning the look of it, but about re-doing the content.

  • Which means the direction shifted.

  • Which means the marketing is changing.

  • Which means the strategy got an overhaul

Last week, I put some of the final touches on the new theme. It is not perfect, and their is a lot of content on the calendar to get to, but the stage is set to execute on the new strategy.

Like every ecommerce website, this is a never ending project. And we are fortunate that wrapping up this Mouse Whisperer series coincides with the launch of this new strategy, because now we get to take that journey together.

That is what I will be writing about once a week, along with comics and other random CRO or customer psychology topics.
