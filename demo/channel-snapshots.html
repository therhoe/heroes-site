How channel traffic is measured

Snapshot tracking counts an acquisition session once and keeps its arrival channel across internal page views. A fresh external or campaign arrival, or 30 minutes of inactivity, starts a new session. Historical traffic is not imported.

Human sessions are Real + Zombie. Conversion rate uses converted human sessions ÷ human sessions; real traffic share uses Real ÷ all sessions. These are Snapshot metrics, not Shopify Analytics. The graph shows one capture only, with conversions assigned to the session’s first captured time bucket. Channel and page snapshots share the existing snapshot allowance.
