PACE framework

This is rooted in the "Jobs To Be Done" concept:

The Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework is a customer-centric mindset that states people do not just buy products; they "hire" them to make progress and solve specific problems in their lives. (credit to Clayton Christensen)

Framing JTBD for product types:

  • Problem -- Solve
  • Pain Point -- Relieve
  • Promise -- Attract

Based on the product type, you will seek out information in various ways, or a brand will be seeking you out. This can be on social, search, ads, friend recommendations, forums, articles & media...
