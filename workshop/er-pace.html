PACE Expert Review

// expert review & roadmaps

PACE gives the review its structure: four questions, in the order the customer lives them. On each stage you'll take notes and rate how urgently it needs CRO work.

P // Prognosis"Will this get me the outcome I want?"
A // Activity"Is this what I clicked on?"
C // Criteria"Do I know enough to decide?"
E // EventsWhat do they actually click?

P, A, and C are your expert opinion. E is where you check those opinions against what customers actually do.
