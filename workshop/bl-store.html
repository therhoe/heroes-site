how a pro reads this

Two numbers, multiplied. Everything else is detail.

trafficThe ceiling on what a win is worth. A page with 42 sessions cannot pay for the week you'd spend on it, however broken it is.
frictionThe room there is to win. A page already converting at 3.4% with 64% scroll depth has told you it works.

Neither alone is an answer. High traffic and low friction is a page doing its job — leave it. High friction and low traffic is a page nobody is harmed by. You want the overlap: enough people to matter, enough breakage to fix.

Zombie percentage is the quiet one. Sessions that arrived and did nothing — no scroll, no click, nothing. A page with 28% zombies has a quarter of its traffic lying about how it's doing, in both directions.
