Estimating revenue lift

// the backlog

Here is a simple way to estimate the lift in revenue to score which page will have a higher impact when increasing revenue is the primary goal.

It's rough, not accurate. Use it to point the team at the right page — not as a number for a report.

the calculation

Snapshot gives you sessions, CVR and revenue per page. Three steps turn those into an estimated gain.

  • orders = sessions × CVR — 336 × 7.7% = 26 orders
  • revenue per order = revenue ÷ orders — $1,845 ÷ 26 = $70.96
  • pick the lift you think is realistic, and apply it — +10% of 26 = 2.6 more orders
  • estimated gain = extra orders × revenue per order — 2.6 × $70.96 = $184.50

the shortcut

A 10% lift on orders is a 10% lift on that page's revenue, so the estimated gain is just revenue × your lift. The per-order figure sets the size of the number, never the ranking — if revenue is your goal, the revenue column already tells you the order.

Working it out the long way is still worth it: the order count is what tells you whether a test on that page could ever reach significance.

two things it is not

  • revenue per order is not AOV — Snapshot splits each order's total across the distinct products in it, so a page's share sits below the real basket value
  • a bigger estimate is not a better bet — it says nothing about whether the lift is achievable, or testable on that page's traffic
